WELCOME TO AETHERGLOW

An Orientation for New Arrivals

Issued by the Argent Council

If you are reading this, you have arrived at

Suratanivar — an elven city of learning,

discipline, and uneasy vigilance.


Whether by intention, curiosity, or chance,

you now stand within the world of Aetherglow.


Take a moment.

You are not expected to know everything.

You are only expected to choose what kind of

life — and what kind of risks — you wish to take.

WHAT IS AETHERGLOW?

Aetherglow is a high-fantasy world shaped by ancient catastrophe and sustained by fragile balance. Long ago, the world was sealed behind a vast arcane boundary known as the Thaumosphere. This barrier protects Aetherglow from full extraplanar invasion — but it also traps magic, souls, and power within the world itself.

The result is a land where:

  • Magic is everywhere, and never entirely safe
  • Power leaves scars — on people and places
  • “Gods” walk the world, but none are untouchable
  • Civilizations endure through vigilance, not fate
This is not a world waiting to be saved by destiny. It is a world shaped by those who show up.
WHERE YOU ARE NOW

You begin near the Argent Council’s training compound on the southern peninsula of Il’Vaé Àêl’Rava, close to the elven city of Suratanivar.

The Argent Council is an organization devoted to confronting existential threats — dangers that ordinary armies and rulers cannot handle alone.

They train operatives.

They investigate corruption.

They intervene when the alternative is collapse.

Not everyone trusts the Council.

Even fewer understand it fully.

But for now, this is a place of relative safety.

THE STATE OF THE WORLD

Far to the north, a dark power stirs.

A corrupted demigod known as Zhaiûrêil — the Corrupted King — has begun pushing his forces south through the mountains.

Villages beyond the frontier have fallen. Forts strain under constant assault. The river town of Falconwold now stands as the last strong settlement before the northern wilds.

This conflict is ongoing.

Its outcome is not predetermined.

Your actions — or your absence — matter.

CHOOSING YOUR PATH

There is no single way to exist in Aetherglow. Most new arrivals naturally fall into one of three broad paths:

  • The Argent Council Train, study, and take part in organized operations against corruption and invasion. This path suits those who enjoy structure, missions, and coordinated effort.
  • Falconwold and the Frontier Live as a civilian, trader, farmer, mercenary, or independent adventurer. This path focuses on town life, survival, relationships, and smaller-scale stories.
  • The Corrupted Swear allegiance to Zhaiûrêil and take part in antagonist-side roleplay. This path explores darker perspectives and direct opposition to the rest of the world.

These are starting points, not permanent cages.

Loyalties may change.

Stories evolve.

ABOUT ROLEPLAY HERE

This is a roleplay-focused environment.


You do not need to be an expert writer.

You do not need to know every piece of lore.

You do not need to roleplay combat perfectly.

You are expected to:

  • Separate in-character (IC) and out-of-character (OOC)/
  • Communicate clearly and in good faith/
  • Respect other players’ boundaries/
  • Treat the shared story as collaborative/

Second Life mechanics are tools — not the story itself. If something mechanical is unclear, ask. Help is always preferable to confusion.

PRACTICAL NOTES (OUT OF CHARACTER)

• This is a developing sim; some areas are unfinished or evolving

• Events, quests, and storylines will expand over time

• Not everything is scripted — player initiative is encouraged

• If you are new to roleplay, that is okay

If you are unsure where to start, remaining near the Argent Council compound or traveling to Falconwold is usually a good first step.

FINAL WORD

Aetherglow is not a theme park.

It is a living setting shaped by its people.

You are not required to be heroic.

You are not forbidden from being dangerous.

You are only asked to be present.

Choose your path.

Step forward when ready.

The world will respond.
