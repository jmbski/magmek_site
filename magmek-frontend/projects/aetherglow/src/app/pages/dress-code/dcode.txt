
1. Core Principle
All avatars and attire should align with the medieval fantasy tone of Aetherglow.
This is a high-magic, high-fantasy world, allowing for:
Magical constructs
Exotic species
Arcane or pseudo-scientific designs (especially elven-influenced)
All appearances must remain visually and thematically plausible within a fantasy setting.

2. In-Character (IC) Appearance Guidelines
2.1 Fantasy Theme Requirement
Avatars must visually fit within a medieval fantasy setting.
Allowed examples:
Knights, rogues, mages, nobles
Fantasy races (elves, orcs, tieflings, etc.)
Anthro or beastfolk (fantasy-styled)
Golems, constructs, or arcane beings
Not allowed:
Sci-fi elements without fantasy reinterpretation
 (e.g., space suits, futuristic armor, battle droids)
Clearly modern or real-world aesthetics that break immersion

2.2 Modern Clothing (Limited Allowance)










2.3 Constructs & Mechanical Avatars
Mechanical or robotic avatars must be adapted to a fantasy context.
Allowed:
Arcane constructs
Clockwork automatons
Animated armor
Not allowed:
Futuristic robots or androids with modern/sci-fi design language

2.4 Stylized / Cartoon Avatars
Avatars with highly stylized or non-realistic aesthetics may be restricted if they disrupt immersion.
Generally discouraged or disallowed:
Anime-style avatars with exaggerated proportions
Cartoon or franchise-based avatars (e.g., My Little Pony-style)
Note:
 Exceptions may be allowed if the avatar is clearly adapted to fit the fantasy aesthetic.

3. Avatar Body & Species Policy
3.1 Allowed Body Types
Aetherglow supports a wide range of species and forms:
Humans and humanoids
Anthro/furry avatars (fantasy-styled)
Creatures and monsters
Exotic or non-standard beings
Requirement: Must still read as fantasy, not sci-fi or parody.

3.2 Child Avatar Policy (Strict)
Child avatars are not permitted.
This ensures:
Compliance with sim rating and platform rules
Clear boundaries in both public and private interactions
Definition of a Child Avatar
Determined by overall presentation, not just height.
Indicators include:
Proportions: Large head, short limbs, small frame
Facial features: Youthful textures, large eyes, soft structure
Body development: Lack of adult characteristics
Styling: Childlike clothing, animations, or accessories
Clarification
Short races (e.g., gnomes, halflings) are allowed
They must be clearly presented as adults
Final determination is at staff discretion.

4. Public Decency & Content Rules
4.1 General Rule
Public areas must remain non-explicit and appropriate for a Moderate-rated environment.

4.2 Nudity
Genitals must not be visible in public spaces
Applies to all avatars equally
Toplessness:
Not inherently considered sexual
Treated consistently regardless of avatar type

4.3 Sexual / Fetish Attire
Not allowed in public areas:
Explicit sexual attachments
Fetish gear intended for sexual activity
 (e.g., gags, BDSM equipment, explicit accessories)
Allowed:
Fantasy armor or revealing clothing that is not explicitly sexual in purpose

4.4 Weapons & Equipment
Weapons and carried equipment must fit the fantasy setting.
Allowed:
Swords, bows, staves, shields
Magical or arcane weaponry
Not allowed:
Firearms
Modern or futuristic weapons
Explosives or military equipment with no fantasy equivalent

4.5 Logos, Branding & Text
Modern or real-world references are not allowed on avatars or clothing.
Not allowed:
Brand logos (e.g., Nike, Adidas, etc.)
Real-world text or slogans
Pop culture or franchise imagery
Allowed:
Fantasy symbols, crests, or fictional insignias

4.6 Private Spaces
Private/residential areas allow greater flexibility, but must still comply with:
Sim rating (Moderate)
Second Life Terms of Service
Child avatar prohibition

5. Out-of-Character (OOC) Visitors
OOC visitors are exempt from fantasy theme requirements only.
They must still follow:
Nudity rules
Sexual content restrictions
Child avatar prohibition
Logos/branding restrictions

6. Enforcement & Discretion
These rules exist to maintain immersion, consistency, and safety
Edge cases will be handled at staff discretion
If unsure, players are encouraged to ask before entering the sim
